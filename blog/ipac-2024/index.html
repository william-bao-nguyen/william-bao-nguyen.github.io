IPAC ’24 in Nashville

I presented my work benchmarking beam–beam physics modules in WarpX at the International Particle Accelerator Conference in Nashville. The project resulted in both a conference proceeding and a poster presentation.

A highlight was speaking with WarpX developers and reconnecting with other former FACET-II summer interns from SLAC.
