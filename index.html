Research Overview

Bao's journey into plasma physics started at Imperial College London, where he worked on laser wakefield accelerator lab automation under Professor Zulfikar Najmudin. It was here that he was first introduced to different plasma diagnostics and particle-in-cell (PIC) codes, which he later worked on in several other projects.

In the summer of his second year, he did a SULI internship at SLAC National Lab under Professor Spencer Gessner. He worked on benchmarking the PIC code WarpX for linear collider applications.

During his exchange year at MIT, Bao learned more about the milestone ignition achievement at NIF and started to develop an interest in fusion energy. Here, he worked under Dr. Maria Gatu Johnson at the High Energy Density Physics Division to simulate modulated deuteron spectra observed by the MRS.

Last summer, Bao worked at Realta Fusion under Dr. Kai Shih to develop PIC code WarpX for start-to-end simulation of neutral-beam-injection (NBI) devices.

Outside of plasma physics, Bao briefly worked on computational chemistry at the University of Medicine & Pharmacy HCMC in Vietnam, running molecular dynamics simulations to evaluate the potential of different molecules in preventing antibiotic resistance in bacteria.
