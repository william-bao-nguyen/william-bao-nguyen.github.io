About

I am interested in using simulation and inference to understand extreme physical systems—and in making those tools clear, reproducible, and useful.

I completed an integrated MSci in Physics at Imperial College London, including an exchange year at MIT. My research has taken me from laser–plasma experiments and accelerator physics to fusion diagnostics and neutral-beam injection.

Outside research, I enjoy anime and cooking Vietnamese dishes that connect me to my childhood in Ho Chi Minh City.
